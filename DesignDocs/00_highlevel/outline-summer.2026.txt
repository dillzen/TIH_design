summon the consciousness of the dead and question them what happened. 

Within the futuristic city of Paracity, he is just a contractor because his job is not considered proper police work anymore. 

One day he is called to a murder scene, and something strange happens. It is an intentional murder, which is almost unheard of by now. 
There is also something wrong, when the real police attempt to summon and question the conscioussnes. 

Together with his intern (characteristics) he sets out to investigate an do old fashioned detective work. 


His investigation leads him first to

- Ezra, a mother who has contracted a company to put her son into cryostasis, in order to prevent something terrible happening to him.
Social services are taking her son away.

- COMPANY that promises cryostasis for terminally ill, and those wishing to wake up in a better future
